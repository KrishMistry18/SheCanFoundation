Who We Are

A movement rooted in
belief and action

She Can Foundation is an NGO registered under the Indian Society Registration Act of 1860, built on one core belief — that no woman’s potential should be limited by the circumstances of her birth.

We work at the intersection of education, healthcare, and economic opportunity to create lasting change in the lives of underprivileged women and girls across India.

“The next time you spend ₹300 on coffee, remember: that same amount gives 3 girls sanitary pads for an entire month.”

From distributing dignity kits in rural communities to running vocational training workshops, every action we take is rooted in the belief that empowered women transform not just families, but entire communities.
